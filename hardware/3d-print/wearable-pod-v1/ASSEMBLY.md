# Assembly, Opening, and Closing

## Purchased hardware

- Four M2 × 6 mm button-head screws for the top lid.
- Four M2 heat-set inserts, approximately 3.2 mm outside diameter and 3 mm long, selected using the tolerance coupon.
- Two M2.5 × 6 mm low-profile screws for the strap plate.
- Two matching M2.5 heat-set inserts, approximately 3.7 mm outside diameter.
- Two M2 thread-forming screws for the removable electronics carrier.
- 25 mm wide soft hook-and-loop strap with a breakaway overlap.
- 8 mm removable battery-retaining strap.
- Thin closed-cell foam wearer pad and battery insulation.

Do not substitute fasteners until their head, length, thread engagement, and internal clearance have been checked in CAD and in a transparent or empty test assembly.

## Heat-set inserts

1. Print and test the tolerance coupon.
2. Support the boss from below on a flat surface.
3. Use a temperature-controlled insertion tip sized for the insert.
4. Press vertically without forcing or twisting the boss.
5. Stop when the insert is flush; do not push it into the battery cavity.
6. Let the plastic cool fully before inserting a screw.
7. Reject a boss that is cracked, distorted, loose, or visibly tilted.

Install four M2 inserts from the open top of the base. Install the two strap-mount inserts from the wearer-facing underside.

## Electronics carrier

1. Measure the finished battery. It must fit without bending, pinching, or compressing the pouch cell.
2. Add electrical insulation between the cell and every PCB/solder surface.
3. Place the battery in the left pocket and retain it with an 8 mm removable fabric strap through the two carrier slots. Do not use a rigid clamp across the cell.
4. Slide the XIAO board into the right-side rails with USB-C toward the side opening.
5. Route the external antenna away from the battery, vibration motor, metal screws, and the wearer’s body. Do not coil the antenna lead tightly.
6. Route wires with service loops and strain relief. No wire may cross a screw boss, shell tongue, gasket, button plunger, or sharp printed edge.
7. Attach the carrier using two M2 thread-forming screws. Tighten by hand only.

## Button and lid stack

1. Place the translucent light ring into the lid’s upper annular recess.
2. Insert the rigid button cap from the underside of the lid. Its larger flange must remain below the lid opening.
3. Flip the TPU membrane from its print orientation so its plunger points down toward the tactile switch.
4. Place the membrane under the cap and align its four screw holes.
5. Confirm that the cap moves freely approximately 0.8 mm and returns without rubbing.
6. Adjust the physical switch/actuator height with a designed spacer—not loose tape—if the switch is not actuated consistently across the full cap.

## Closing the enclosure

1. Power the pod off and disconnect USB.
2. Confirm the battery is secure and undamaged.
3. Inspect the tongue, groove, gasket recess, wire routes, and screw bosses for debris.
4. Lay the TPU perimeter gasket flat in the base recess. Do not stretch or twist it.
5. Lower the completed lid vertically so the tongue enters the groove. Never trap a wire under the sealing surface.
6. Insert all four M2 screws and turn each one only two rotations.
7. Tighten gradually in a diagonal cross pattern until the gap is uniform and the gasket is lightly compressed.
8. Do not use a powered screwdriver and do not crush the printed lid.
9. Press the cap at its center and around four edges. It must return every time.
10. Run button, LED, vibration, radio, charging, and power-switch tests before fitting the strap.

## Opening the enclosure

1. End the game, power the pod off, unplug USB, and wait for all LEDs to turn off.
2. Remove the strap plate if it blocks safe handling.
3. Loosen the four lid screws gradually in a diagonal pattern.
4. Lift the lid vertically using equal finger pressure around the rim.
5. Do not insert a metal screwdriver near the battery. If the gasket sticks, use a broad plastic opening pick only at the external shell seam.
6. Keep screws, gasket, cap, membrane, and light ring together in a clean parts tray.
7. Inspect the cell before touching or moving internal wiring. Stop if it is swollen, hot, leaking, punctured, or smells unusual.

## Attachment and safety

Pass a soft 25 mm strap through both slots in the removable plate. The strap should release under abnormal load; do not use a neck lanyard, permanent zip tie, rigid metal band, or strong magnet. Add a replaceable foam pad between the plate and clothing/body. The two attachment screws must end inside their blind bosses and must never protrude into the electronics cavity.

## Validation before active play

- 100 manual presses at the center and four edges.
- 30-minute powered test while worn.
- USB insertion/removal without shell movement.
- Ten open/close service cycles with no loose inserts or boss cracks.
- One-metre controlled drop testing onto wood and carpet with an inert battery substitute first.
- Strap pull/release test using a documented fixture, not a person’s neck or limb.
- Radio-range test in the actual worn orientation.

This prototype is not approved for unsupervised children or commercial sale.
