# Image prompt set

Mode: OpenAI built-in image generator.

Hardware/style reference: `assets/handbook/kit-concept.png`.

## Shared direction

Each game used the following shared production direction, combined with its scene brief below:

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 16:9 PowerPoint game explainer illustration
Audience: understandable at a glance by a 10-year-old
Input image: the kit concept is a hardware and palette reference for round electronic pods; preserve navy shells and teal, coral, amber, and warm-white lights
Style/medium: polished friendly 3D children's educational illustration, rounded forms, expressive poses, clean modern composition
Color palette: deep navy, teal, coral, amber, warm off-white
Constraints: no written words, no numbers, no logos, no watermark; show safe device use; avoid grabbing, pushing, tackling, dangerous obstacles, or threatening behavior
```

## Selected scene briefs

1. **Pulse Hunt** — Bright garden with a glowing safe boundary. Exactly five players: one Hunter presses only a Runner's upper-back pod, two Runners move safely, and one tagged player raises a hand and walks to a teal safe zone.
2. **Vault Heist** — Two glowing team vault circles. A player safely holds an opponent's arm pod, a gem-shaped light moves between vaults, and a shield ring appears after the steal.
3. **Signal Chain** — Six players in a semicircle. Their pods light in a visible sequence connected by arrows; the first player presses their own pod while the others remember the next turn.
4. **Guardian Link** — Secret pairs are suggested by matching heart-light links. A partner presses the threatened player's pod in time, creating a protective shield that cancels a hit.
5. **Territory Pulse** — Three safe floor zones with detached pods on stable bases. Two teams walk between zones while teal and coral lights show which team controls each area.
6. **Echo Match** — Six players around a central hub that shows a simple three-color pattern. Players copy the pattern on their own pods; a star glow marks the first correct answer.
7. **Silent Mission** — Six players at a friendly gathering, each with one harmless mission symbol in a thought bubble. A central player quietly presses their own wrist pod; a table hub shows completed stars.
8. **Relay Rush** — Six players arranged along a curved route. A glowing signal travels through arrows from one lit pod to the next; a clock icon shows that mistakes add time.
9. **Crown Circuit** — Three-beat visual story: an amber crown marks the holder, a challenger presses the Crown pod, then the two duelists press their own pods after a flash while other pods lock.
10. **Courier Code** — Exactly five seated players. A glowing envelope moves during a secret handoff, the Interceptor safely scans a different pod and gets a miss, and another player performs a decoy.
11. **Double Lock** — Stable table pods and a visual color clue. Two players hold the matching pods simultaneously as progress bars meet and a large lock opens.
12. **Market Rush** — Six seated players trade fictional Spark, Fabric, and Time icons. Two players confirm together, resources swap, and a completed icon-only contract creates a Reputation star.
13. **Power Grid** — Six pods form a generator ring around a glowing miniature city. Players raise output, cool a hot pod, and link two pods for a repair while supply and demand bars match.
14. **Rhythm Rivals** — Six players follow colored beat waves from a central hub. Pods glow before each cue; two players hit a shared team note and earn a perfect-timing star.
15. **Switchback** — Six seated players form a circle. A white Signal sits on the active pod while arrows show short, long, and double-press routes; a reverse icon flips the directions.
16. **Ghost Frequency** — Six players conduct a pair test. The hub returns a green same-result, an icon-only evidence graph connects colored dots, and the hidden Drifter has a small playful static swirl.
